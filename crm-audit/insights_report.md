## 3. Resurrection Scripts

### Timing

**SMS 1 (Low-pressure check-in)**
> "Hey [Name] — saw we were mid-conversation about your growth plan. Did the
> Q3 launch end up going the way you wanted, or is it still eating your
> bandwidth?"

**SMS 2 (Re-open on their terms)**
> "Hey [Name], no pressure at all — just curious if now's a better window to
> pick this back up, or if it makes more sense to check in again next
> month?"

**Email (High-intent)**
> Subject: Still on your radar?
>
> Hey [Name],
>
> Last time we talked, timing was the main thing in the way — you wanted to
> get past [the specific thing] first. Just checking in: has that cleared up
> at all, or is it still in motion?
>
> No need to reply if now isn't the moment. If it *is*, happy to grab 15
> minutes and pick up right where we left off.
>
> [Signature]

### Trust / ROI Confidence

**SMS 1 (Proof, not pressure)**
> "Hey [Name] — thought of you when I saw one of our clients hit [result].
> Wanted to send it over in case it's useful either way."

**SMS 2 (Direct, non-defensive)**
> "Hey [Name], totally fair to want more certainty before committing. What
> would actually need to be true for this to feel like a clear yes for you?"

**Email (High-intent)**
> Subject: The ROI question you raised
>
> Hey [Name],
>
> You mentioned you weren't 100% sure this would pencil out. That's a fair
> thing to want clarity on before committing real money.
>
> Here's what I'd point to: [specific case study, numbers, timeline]. Not
> saying your results would match exactly, but it's the same system.
>
> If it'd help, I'm glad to walk through the actual math for your situation
> — no pitch, just numbers.
>
> [Signature]

### Price / Capital

**SMS 1 (Low-pressure check-in)**
> "Hey [Name] — was just looking back at the plan we sketched out. Did the
> cash flow situation ease up at all, or is that still the main thing?"

**SMS 2 (Asymmetric risk offset)**
> "Hey [Name], quick thought — if we structured it so a smaller amount was
> due upfront and the rest was tied to hitting specific milestones, would
> that change the math for you?"

**Email (High-intent)**
> Subject: A different way to structure this
>
> Hey [Name],
>
> When we last talked, the investment felt like a stretch given where cash
> flow was at. Totally understandable — I'd want the same clarity in your
> position.
>
> One option: split the retainer so less is due upfront, with the balance
> tied to results once the system is live. Happy to walk through what that'd
> look like for you specifically if it's still something you want to solve.
>
> [Signature]
