# Viral Shorts Pack 04: "Did you know in [movie]..." movie trivia and theories

Niche (per the user's description): hidden details, trivia and fan theories about big movies, such as Marvel's Loki and Gamora. Short, punchy, curiosity-driven.

Structure that keeps people watching:
1. **Hook (0-3s):** a bold claim about a movie they know. "Did you know Loki almost wasn't Loki?"
2. **Build (3-30s):** 2-3 details, each one a mini reveal, with a visual change every 2-3 seconds.
3. **Payoff (last 5-8s):** the "wait, that changes everything" line, plus a question to drive comments ("Which one did you miss?").

Format: 9:16, 30-45s, 90-110 spoken words, fast captions, SFX on every cut.

Facts are from general knowledge. (verify) marks the ones to check before posting. Frame fan theories as theories, and trivia as trivia.

On footage: short clips of film scenes can still draw copyright claims. Safer options are your own AI-generated or animated scenes, or very short clips with commentary.

## Short 1: Loki

**Titles:** "Loki Almost Wasn't Loki" / "The Actor Who Wanted Thor Got Loki Instead"

> Did you know the actor who plays Loki originally auditioned for Thor? [0-5s]
> Tom Hiddleston read for the hero role. The studio saw something else in him. [5-13s]
> They gave him Loki, a villain with one scene in the first movie's script. (verify wording) [13-21s]
> And it became the character fans love most. [21-27s]
> Which means the best part of the MCU almost went to the wrong actor. [27-34s]

## Short 2: Gamora

## Posting plan

- Don't present a theory as confirmed fact. Viewers punish wrong claims in comments, which also hurts reach.
