THE AUDIT

Four things costing you sales right now.

Finding 01

One price point catches one type of buyer

$30 is a big ask for someone who's never heard of you, and way too small for someone who wants direct access to you. Right now both of those people get the same offer — or nothing.

Estimated impact: most of your 5.6K followers never convert either direction.
Finding 02

The sales page has no proof on it

No student results, no testimonials, no "X people already in." Just copy and a price. For a cold visitor with no context on you, that's the single biggest thing standing between them and paying.

Estimated impact: conversion rate is likely well under half of what proof alone would add.
Finding 03

Anyone who doesn't buy is gone forever

No email capture, no retargeting, no follow-up. Someone lands on the page, hesitates, leaves — and there's no way to ever reach them again.

Estimated impact: this is the single largest silent leak in the funnel.
Finding 04

No path for your highest-intent buyers

The people most locked in — the ones who'd pay real money for direct access to you — have nowhere to go past the $30 course. That's the highest-margin part of your audience going completely uncaptured.

Estimated impact: this is where most of the missing revenue actually is.
